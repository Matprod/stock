6. Chili Con Carne Recipe

Ingredients (4 servings): 500g ground beef, 1 large onion (diced), 2 cloves garlic (minced), 1 red bell pepper (diced), 400g canned tomatoes, 400g kidney beans (drained), 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, salt & pepper to taste, olive oil.

Instructions: Heat oil in a large pot. Sauté onion and garlic until soft. Add ground beef and cook until browned. Stir in bell pepper, tomato paste, and spices. Add canned tomatoes and kidney beans. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with rice or tortilla chips.
